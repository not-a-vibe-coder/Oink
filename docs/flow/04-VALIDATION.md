# Customer validation checklist

Status: pending external evidence. No interviews, pilot users, or market demand are claimed.

Interview five USDC-paid freelancers. Ask for their most recent payment workflow, cash reserve, investing habits, tools, and concerns about executing an allocation. Show an illustrative cash-first allocation. Ask whether they would use it for their next payment and why.

If accessible, interview five tokenized-stock traders about their last trade, execution surprises, and checks they performed. Ask what a specific-order execution brief would change.

Record consented anonymized findings here: date, customer segment, existing behaviour, pain, proposed change, and concrete commitment. Do not store personal account or financial credentials.

Success is observed configuration and real use, followed by repeat use. Compliments are not validation. Technical development can proceed as an explicitly unvalidated MVP; launch and competition claims must preserve that limitation.
