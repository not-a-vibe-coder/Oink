Oink2 / 8

How Bell protects you

Nothing is bought until
you sign, and only if it passes.

Before the quote reaches you

  • Exact-amount Jupiter Swap v2 route, 30-second expiry
  • Slippage, price-impact and maximum-price limits
  • Premium cap over the Pyth stock price, after the xStock's on-chain share multiplier, only with fresh regular-session data

Before and after you sign

  • Instruction allowlist, decoded route, you as the only signer, simulation
  • Signed locally: the server never holds your key
  • Saved before broadcast; uncertain outcomes are reconciled, never resent

Pass → review and sign. Defer → your USDC stays put. Bell never invents a price.
